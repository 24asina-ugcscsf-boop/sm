Scholarship Schemes

  • Puthumai Penn Thittam

    Financial assistance of ₹1,000/month for female students who studied in Tamil Nadu Government schools from Class 6th to 12th.

  • Central Sector Scheme

    Scholarship for meritorious students based on Class 12th Board Examination marks.

  • Uzhavar Nala Variyam

    Educational assistance provided for children of registered farmers/agricultural workers.

  • SC / ST / SCC Scholarship

    Post-Matric Scholarship and full tuition fee exemption scheme provided by the State Government.

  • BC / MBC / DNC Scholarship

    State Government welfare scholarship scheme for BC, MBC, and DNC category students.
